Virtual case conference

STR-X Conference Platform

One shared room · 3D imaging · later, live ASTRAL holograms

A multi-site virtual case conference (“tumor board”) for complex neurosurgical pre-op planning — tumor, trauma, and stroke. Sites around the world beam in to one shared room to review 3D imaging together and, later, live ASTRAL volumetric holograms from the AI-Surgical Training & Mixed Reality Avatar Laboratory (ASTRAL).

Access

Three ways to join one room

Every site looks at the same case. Choose the view that fits the device in front of you.

Tier 1

Meta Quest mixed reality

Highest immersion. A life-size scan is anchored to the real OR table, with hand tracking, slice panels, and depth occlusion — so the volume sits in the room you are actually standing in.

Tier 2

Browser 3D room

Orbit the table in any modern desktop or phone browser. Avatars mark who is present; a shared pointer marks what the group is looking at.

Tier 3

2D livestream

Fixed spectator cameras — overhead, surgeon’s-eye, and side — for any device or a lecture-hall television. No headset required.

Session flow

How a session works

  1. Invite-only sign-in

    Participants enter with an invited account and multi-factor authentication. There is no open public room for live cases.

  2. Disclosure on join

    A required disclosure screen is shown before the room unlocks, so roles, recording status, and imaging rules are visible at the door.

  3. Imaging rules

    Only de-identified or consented imaging is used. This public page and demo contain no patient information.

  4. Education log

    Attendance minutes and a post-session evaluation are recorded so the activity can support continuing-education workflows if a series is later approved.

Continuing education

Stack

Built on open source

  • OrthancDICOM archive for study storage and retrieval in the live platform.
  • vtk.js · three.js · WebXRBrowser 3D, volume views, and mixed-reality sessions (including this public demo).
  • LiveKitRealtime media for the shared room and 2D spectator livestreams.
  • KeycloakInvite-only identity, roles, and multi-factor authentication.
  • cwipcPoint-cloud capture used for ASTRAL volumetric holograms.
